How we fry

Three things we refuse
to rush

01

Marinate overnight

Every batch of chicken sits in the marinade the night before service. That is where the flavour gets past the crust.

02

Inject the spice blend

Our blend goes into the meat, not just onto it — so the last bite tastes like the first one.

03

Fry to order

Into the fryer only once you have ordered, out at a deep gold. It reaches your table loud and crunchy.
