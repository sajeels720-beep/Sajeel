## Description

```
Stay in the pool too long and your fingers turn into raisins... but it's not just the water. 💦

Water seeping into your skin sets off your nerves, which squeeze the tiny blood vessels in your fingertips. The pads shrink a little and the skin on top crumples into wrinkles. Doctors have used this soak test to check the nerves in the hand: a finger whose nerve is cut doesn't wrinkle. And why? Scientists think the wrinkles help you grip wet things, like the treads on a tyre, though not every study agrees.

Sources:
Wilder-Smith E. & Chow A., "Water-immersion wrinkling is due to vasoconstriction", Muscle & Nerve, 2003
```
