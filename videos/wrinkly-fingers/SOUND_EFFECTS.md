# Sound effects

Every sound the video needs, with a prompt for ElevenLabs' **Sound Effects** tool. Generate each sound once (two or three tries, keep the best), name it as shown and put it in `videos/wrinkly-fingers/audio/` next to the voiceover. Each sound is reused wherever it fits, and the edit places them. The times below are where they go (from the animation), so you don't need to line anything up yourself.

Tips for ElevenLabs Sound Effects:
- Set the duration to the **Length** column (0.5 s is the shortest ElevenLabs makes). Longer is fine: the edit trims them.
- Prompts work best as plain descriptions. Ending with "short, clean" or "no background noise" keeps them usable.
- His sound (`sfx_sigh`) must be a **young man**.
- The free plan has no commercial licence, the same as for the voice. For a monetized channel, use the Starter plan or above.

★ = needed; the rest are nice extras.

## Music

The video needs one background track, about 30 seconds long (set 30 s), that ends on a short "button" note; it plays from 0.0 s and the edit lands its last note at 25.0 s. Use ElevenLabs Music, or a free track from the YouTube Audio Library. Name it `music`. A prompt for ElevenLabs Music:

> Light, sunny, playful background music for a fun science short: plucky ukulele and soft marimba with a gentle bouncy beat, summer pool-day feel, no vocals, about 30 seconds, ending with a short final button note
