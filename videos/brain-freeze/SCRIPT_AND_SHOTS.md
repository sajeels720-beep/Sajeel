# "Why Ice Cream Freezes Your Brain 🥶" — script and shot plan

An original 37-second Short in the style of Zack D. Films ("Why You Can't Run In Your Dreams 😬", 36.6 s), built from a frame-by-frame study of that video.

## His formula (what this script copies, and what it doesn't)

| | His video | This script |
|---|---|---|
| Length / pace | 36.6 s, ~95 words (about 2.6 words per second) | ~37 s, 97 words |
| Structure | Hook in second person ("you") → **"You see,"** explanation → **"But…"** twist → callback to the hook ("noodle legs") | Same beats: bite → pain → "You see" → "But" → callback ("frozen brain") |
| Shots | ~20 shots, 1–2.5 s each, camera always moving | 19 shots, 1.5–2.6 s |
| Visual devices | Extreme close-ups, brain cutaway with electric arcs, X-ray muscles, red ripple rings, red "?" icon, thought-bubble transitions | Cutaway brain with frost, X-ray blood vessels, red pain pulses on a nerve, red "?" icon, callback shot |
| Look | 9:16, 4K at 30 fps; bright saturated colours, soft sun, shallow depth of field, slight chromatic aberration | Same |
| Captions | 2–4 words, white bold sans-serif with a soft shadow, centred at ~75% height, synced to each word group | Same |

The story and wording are new. Only the format and pacing follow his video.

## Script (timed)

| Time | Voiceover |
|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0 | You take a giant bite of ice cream, |
| 3.0–5.2 | and for a second, it's perfect. |
| 5.6–8.4 | Then a stabbing pain shoots through your forehead, |
| 8.4–10.6 | like your brain is freezing solid. |
| 11.0–14.8 | **You see,** when something freezing hits the roof of your mouth, |
| 14.8–18.8 | the blood vessels in your head squeeze tight to hold in heat, |
| 18.8–22.0 | then suddenly rush wide open to warm back up, |
| 22.0–25.4 | and that flood of blood sets off pain sensors. |
| 25.9–29.6 | **But** the nerve carrying that signal also runs to your forehead, |
| 29.6–32.0 | so that's where your brain feels it, |
| 32.0–36.8 | leaving you with a frozen brain that isn't frozen at all. |

Science note: this is the simplified, widely reported explanation. Cold on the palate, followed by vessels narrowing and then rapidly widening (a 2012 study linked the pain to the artery at the front of the brain widening), with the pain carried by the trigeminal nerve and felt in the forehead. Researchers think nerve signalling and blood-flow changes work together.

**Feasibility:** 14 of the 19 shots are objects, anatomy and effects, which suit Claude in Blender well. Shots 3, 4 and 19 need a human with facial expressions, which is the hardest part. Options: a free human generator (MPFB/MakeHuman for Blender) with simple expression controls, keeping those shots short and close (eyes, forehead) as planned.

## Production checklist

- **Format:** vertical 9:16, render 1080×1920 (upscale to 4K optional), 30 fps, ~37 s.
- **Look:** bright saturated colours, soft sun with long shadows, sky HDRI (Poly Haven), shallow depth of field, motion blur, light chromatic aberration and grain.
- **Assets:** ice cream and cup (procedural), park bench and props (Poly Haven), head/skull/brain anatomy (free CC-licensed models, e.g. Sketchfab, needs your login; or stylised models built in Blender), vessels and nerves (procedural curves with glowing materials).
- **Voiceover:** record it yourself (best), or use an AI voice through your connected Everygen/Higgsfield tools (costs credits).
- **Sound:** spoon scrape, whooshes, sting, ice crackle, heartbeat, electric zaps (Freesound, check each licence), plus light background music.
- **Captions:** 2–4 words each, white bold sans-serif with a soft shadow, ~75% down the frame, timed to the voiceover.
- **Title:** "Why Ice Cream Freezes Your Brain 🥶"
