# Voiceover lines and file names

Record one audio file per line and name it as shown (`.wav` is best; `.mp3` or `.m4a` also work). Put them all in one folder, for example `voiceover/`. Each file is placed at its time slot in the edit and plays over the shots listed.

## How it should sound

Like his narrator: a friendly, curious voice telling you a cool fact, brisk and even (about 2.6 words per second), with a little drama on the pain and a playful smile on the last line. Commas are quick breaths, `...` is a short pause, and `—` is a sudden break. Stress the **bold** word or words. If you use an AI voice, paste the text as written, without the bold; the punctuation already shapes the delivery.

| File | Read it like this | Stress | Feel | Time slot (s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `voiceover_01` | You take a giant bite of ice cream... | **giant** | casual, inviting | 0.0–3.0 |
| `voiceover_02` | and for a second... it's perfect. | **perfect** | warm, satisfied, a little drawn out | 3.0–5.2 |
| `voiceover_03` | Then — a stabbing pain shoots through your forehead, | **stabbing** | sudden, sharper, faster | 5.6–8.4 |
| `voiceover_04` | like your brain is freezing solid. | **freezing solid** | dramatic, slightly slower | 8.4–10.6 |
| `voiceover_05` | You see, when something freezing hits the roof of your mouth, | **You see**, **roof** | calm, "here's the secret" | 11.0–14.8 |
| `voiceover_06` | the blood vessels in your head squeeze tight, to hold in heat, | **squeeze tight** | tense, tightening | 14.8–18.8 |
| `voiceover_07` | then suddenly — rush wide open, to warm back up, | **suddenly**, **wide open** | quick burst of energy | 18.8–22.0 |
| `voiceover_08` | and that flood of blood... sets off pain sensors. | **flood**, **pain** | punchy, the pay-off | 22.0–25.4 |
| `voiceover_09` | But, the nerve carrying that signal also runs to your forehead, | **But**, **forehead** | the twist, curious | 25.9–29.6 |
| `voiceover_10` | so that's where your brain feels it, | **that's** | "aha", matter-of-fact | 29.6–32.0 |
| `voiceover_11` | leaving you with a frozen brain... that isn't frozen at all. | **isn't**, **at all** | playful, a smile in the voice | 32.0–36.8 |

The gaps at 5.2–5.6, 10.6–11.0 and 25.4–25.9 s are deliberate beats before "Then", "You see" and "But".

What each line plays over: 1 = shots 1–2 (the bite), 2 = shot 3 (bliss), 3 = shots 4–5 (crash zoom, ice cracks), 4 = shot 6 (brain freezes), 5 = shots 7–8 (x-ray head, frost on the palate), 6 = shots 9–10 (vessels squeeze), 7 = shots 11–12 (vessels burst open, warm melt), 8 = shots 13–14 (blood rush, pain sparks), 9 = shots 15–16 (nerve signal to the forehead), 10 = shot 17 (red "?"), 11 = shots 18–19b (frozen brain, ice shatters, another bite).

Tips:
- If a line comes out longer or shorter than its slot, that's fine: the edit is adjusted to your timing.
- A little silence at the start or end of a file is fine; it gets trimmed.
- Recorded everything in one take? Name it `voiceover_full` and leave a short pause between lines, so it can be split.
