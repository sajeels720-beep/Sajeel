## Description

Holding in a sneeze feels polite… but here's where all that air goes. 🤧

A sneeze blasts air out of your lungs super fast. Pinch your nose and shut your mouth, and the pressure in your airways can jump about 20 times higher. Doctors warn it can burst an eardrum or pop blood vessels in your eyes, and in rare cases it has even torn a hole in someone's throat. So next time, just let it out (into a tissue or your elbow).

Sources:
Misirovs R. et al., "Spontaneous tracheal perforation following a sneeze", BMJ Case Reports, 2023
Yang W. et al., "Snap, crackle and pop: when sneezing leads to crackling in the neck", BMJ Case Reports, 2018
