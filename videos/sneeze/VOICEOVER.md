# Voiceover lines and file names

Record one audio file per line and name it as shown (`.wav` is best; `.mp3` or `.m4a` also work). Put them all in `videos/sneeze/audio/`. The edit fits every shot to your recording, so the times are a guide, not a rule.

Read it like his narrator: friendly, curious and brisk (about 2.7 words per second). Add a bit of drama on "Big mistake" and the pops, and a smile on the last line. Commas are quick breaths, `...` is a short pause, and `—` is a sudden break. Stress the **bold** words.

Tips:
- If a line comes out longer or shorter than its slot, that's fine: the edit is adjusted to your timing.
- A little silence at the start or end of a file is fine; it gets trimmed.
- Recorded everything in one take? Name it `voiceover_full` and leave a short pause between lines.
