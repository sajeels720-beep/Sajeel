## Description
He had 90 days. Plenty of time, he thought.
Then the phone started whispering: just five minutes. you have time. start tomorrow.
At 5 AM, he saw who was paying for every day he wasted.

90 DAYS LEFT. is a short animated film about procrastination, discipline and the people who quietly fight for us.

The result was written on the days nobody saw.
