# Thumbnail prompt (Gemini app / ChatGPT)

Attach two images, in this order: `output/ninety_days_left/thumbnail.png` (the layout) and
`studio/assets/reference/thumbnail_reference.png` (the style). The prompt also works on its own.

```
Create a YouTube thumbnail, 16:9 (1280x720), as a still from a premium 2D animated short film.

If I attached images: use the FIRST for the layout and the character, and the SECOND only for the style and finish. Copy how the second image is drawn and lit, never what it shows.

SCENE: Late at night. A student sits at a wooden desk in a dark bedroom, quietly worried, holding his phone. Behind him a huge mountain of textbooks rises almost to the ceiling.

CHARACTER (keep this exact design): a simple cartoon person with a perfectly round, smooth, off-white head. No hair, no hat, no ears, no nose. Black dot eyes with tiny white catchlights, thick dark eyebrows, a small mouth. He wears a dusty teal-blue hoodie. Clean thick dark outlines, soft shading on the round head.

EMOTION: quiet, human worry, not cartoon panic. The inner ends of his eyebrows are slightly raised, a small frown, his eyes glance down at the phone. No sweat drops, no shake lines, no open mouth.

COMPOSITION:
- His head is big, about a third of the frame height, just right of centre.
- The phone is in his hand, held low in front of him, with its screen facing the viewer. The dark red screen shows two glowing, slanted, angry red eyes, as if a shadow creature lives inside the phone. It throws red light up onto his face and hand.
- Behind him, slightly out of focus: towering stacks of textbooks with muted coloured spines, and a wall calendar with one date circled in red.
- Foreground: a blurred stack of books in the bottom-right corner, and the edge of the desk along the bottom.
- Keep the bottom-right corner free of anything important.

LIGHT AND COLOUR: a near-black navy room, cool moonlight haze from the left, a warm desk-lamp glow on the right side of his face, and the red glow of the phone as the only strong accent colour. Soft cinematic lighting, a gentle haze, shallow depth of field, high contrast. The title and his face are the brightest things in the image.

TITLE: exactly "90 DAYS" on the first line and "LEFT." on the second line, top-left, huge (each line about 40% of the frame width), in bold white rough dry-brush capital letters with a thick black outline and a soft drop shadow. The text must not cover his face.

Do not add any other text, numbers, labels, logos or watermarks. No realistic people, no hair, no hat, no purple or pink tints. It must read clearly at small phone size.
```
