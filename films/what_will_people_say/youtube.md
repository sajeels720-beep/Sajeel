## Description
He had a song in him. He only ever played it alone.
Every time he tried, the voices came back: cringe. who does he think he is? everyone's gonna laugh.

WHAT WILL PEOPLE SAY? is a short animated film about the crowd that lives in your head, and the day you finally play anyway.

Their opinion lasts a minute. Your regret lasts a lifetime.
