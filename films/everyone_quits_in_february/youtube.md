## Description
Every January, everyone starts.
By February, almost everyone is gone.
Something taps them on the shoulder: you're sore. start again monday. nobody's watching.

EVERYONE QUITS IN FEBRUARY. is a short animated film about New Year's resolutions, discipline, and the people who quietly stay.

Everyone starts. Almost nobody stays.

0:00 Happy New Year
0:20 The tap on the shoulder
0:44 February 12th
0:58 Same corner. Every year.
1:12 He stays
1:46 "You've done enough."
2:02 January 1st, again

Every frame and every note of music in this film was made from scratch.

## When to post
The timing is built into the story, so post it in the last week of December, before people make their
resolutions: Monday 28 December, 3 PM New York time (Tuesday 29 December, 1:00 AM in Pakistan; New York is on
winter time by then, so it's 1 AM, not midnight).

Then push it again twice:
- Quitter's Day, the second Friday of January (Friday 8 January 2027), when most resolutions are dropped:
  a community post or a Short cut from the six taps (0:20-0:44).
- Early February, when the film's title comes true.
