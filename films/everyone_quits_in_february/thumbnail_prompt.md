```
Create a YouTube thumbnail, 16:9 (1280x720), as a still from a premium 2D animated short film.

SCENE: February, late at night, in an almost empty gym. Behind him, out of focus, the last few people in matching yellow "NEW ME" t-shirts are dissolving into grey smoke from the feet up. CHARACTER (keep this exact design): a simple cartoon person with a perfectly round, smooth, off-white head. No hair, no hat, no ears, no nose. EMOTION: quiet, human doubt, not cartoon panic. The inner ends of his eyebrows are slightly raised, a small frown, his eyes glance down and to the side. No sweat drops, no shake lines, no open mouth.

COMPOSITION:

- Right behind him looms a tall, pitch-black shadow figure with a soft red rim light and two narrow glowing red eyes. Its black hand rests on his shoulder.
- The dissolving people and the banner are on the left half, blurred, darker than him.

TITLE: exactly "EVERYONE" on the first line and "QUITS." on the second line, top-left, huge (each line about 40% of the frame width), in bold white rough dry-brush capital letters with a thick black outline and a soft drop shadow. The text must not cover his face.

Do not add any other text, numbers, labels, logos or watermarks (the "NEW ME" shirt print and the blurred banner are the only other words). No realistic people, no hair, no hat, no purple or pink tints. It must read clearly at small phone size.
```
